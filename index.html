Get Food

Need food? You don't have to figure it out alone.

Getting groceries is not something to be ashamed of. There are several ways to get food near you, and we'll help you find the right one.

Pantry locator

Find a partner pantry close to home, work, or school anywhere in Lake and Porter counties.

Mobile Market

Fresh food brought out into the community, so you can pick up groceries closer to where you live.

Order Ahead lockers

Place an order and pick it up from a locker on your schedule. Simple and private.

Talk to a real person

Not sure where to start? Call us. We can point you to a pantry, help with SNAP questions, or connect you with a program that fits your family.

About Us

Born in the Region, built by neighbors.

We're the only food bank serving Lake and Porter counties. The idea came home from a 1981 Indianapolis conference with United Way and Steelworker representatives, and it's been growing ever since.

  • 1982Founded, starting with salvaged food.
  • 1989Began accepting donations from retailers.
  • The barn yearsOperated for years from a 12,000-square-foot barn.
  • 2018Moved into a larger distribution facility in Merrillville.
Feeding America member 501(c)(3) nonprofit ★ 4.5 on Google · 276 reviews
